## Dedication

For **Keith Bailey** (1970–2020): mentor, friend, champion of eLearning in Penn State's College of Arts and Architecture, where ELMS began.

He had a phrase for the moment the requirements were finally clear: **"It's as good as coded."** Sometimes it was a jab. Usually it was the highest compliment he gave. It meant: you listened well enough that building it is now the easy part.

> _Bryan: say what Keith was to you, in your words._

## The question

Bill Rose, who worked alongside Keith for about ten years, once stopped and asked: *I wonder what Keith would think of all that you've made.*

> _Bryan: answer this. Even a paragraph. It is probably the best opening this file could have._
