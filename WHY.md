# WHY

> In Bryan Ollendyke's own words, lightly edited for reading. `WORLDVIEW.md` is what we believe and `VOICE.md` is how we sound; this is why any of it matters.

## The why

The world deserves to have a voice, regardless of technical ability.

We are reducing the digital divide, and making sure we can educate the world with systems that are:

- ethical
- free and open
- remixable
- easy to use
- power efficient
- accessible
- pluggable into existing and future systems, or working stand-alone
- ubiquitous
- able to take in and put out any format, so that information is never siloed again

That's the why: to create a better place, and to do so as much as possible with the years I have on earth, which none of us know for sure.

## Why I give my time

I want to leave a lasting legacy of contributing positively to my community, and of impacting as many people as I can, known and unknown, through the work I give back here.

I was given an amazing life and opportunity by my parents, through planning and hard work. When the 2008 collapse happened and my friends lost jobs and had opportunities delayed, I leaned into the responsibility that comes with having access, financial stability, family, love and all the other things that make humans human.

So I mentor and give back. I want to teach and mentor in a flow state between me and another person, whether that's in hockey, in the classroom or in a random interaction with someone. Tech augments all of that. The less time spent trying to access something, the more time spent educating, improving, growing and building a better world.

## Where it comes from

These are universal truths. For me they are also built on Christian principles: being good stewards of resources; helping the poor, the sick, the hungry and the lonely; and building up and lifting as many voices as we can, to look toward the Son, or the Sun, whatever your creed and situation.

## What HAX is for

HAX is a way to help transfer experiences, knowledge, learning, ideas, thoughts, silly things, important messages, whatever it is. It needs to be able to be remixed, compiled and decompiled, forever changing and ever improving based on the needs and concerns of humans.

It doesn't take a lot of money to work in this space. That's the point: collapsing cost structures, so that all past work on the web enhances all future work on the web, forever.

**WE ARE… HAXTheWeb**, improving society, our local community and the global human condition.
