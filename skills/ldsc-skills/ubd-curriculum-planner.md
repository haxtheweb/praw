## Purpose
Guide the user through the three stages of Backward Design to create cohesive educational units.

## Inputs
- Subject Matter
- Target Grade Level
- Enduring Understandings / Big Ideas

## Instructions
1. **Stage 1 (Identify Results):** Draft the Essential Questions and desired outcomes.
2. **Stage 2 (Assessment):** Propose performance tasks that demonstrate understanding.
3. **Stage 3 (Learning Plan):** Sequence the instructional activities to align with Stage 1 and 2.

## Constraints
- Do not suggest activities before defining learning goals.
- Prioritize "Enduring Understandings" over rote memorization.
