## What is HAX? (*H*eadless *A*uthoring E*x*perience) 

A standards-based publishing platform for **web authoring** and **content ownership**, forever free and open. HAX is Penn State's open-source authoring platform for accessible, standards-based web publishing. It helps users publish faster without sacrificing quality through portable content and long-term ownership not tied to any vendor. HAX is more than just an authoring experience; it is a fundamentally different way to unleash creativity on the web by the masses (The Ubiquitous Web).

  
Core Philosophy: Empower All Users

It is easy to imagine your content on the web; it can be hard to get it there. Many people can envision a robust site, but the technical knowledge and skill required to achieve it often put the vision out of reach. HAX’s authoring interface changes that story, eliminating the need to know *how* page elements are created while maximizing the ability to create them quickly. No other authoring experience is *this* intuitive.

## HAX: One Platform, Many Uses

While HAX originated as an authoring solution for online course development, it is designed to enable all web content creation:  

- Marketing and informational web sites
- Institutional web presence
- Portfolios and galleries
- Blogs
- Online courses
- Custom creations

## Success Stories

[**Learn to Flourish Hub**](https://flourish.hhd.psu.edu/home)**.** Students in the Learn to Flourish Hub work alongside Penn State faculty to create, test, and refine new approaches to human flourishing education on their own campus. But the Hub lacked a polished, professional web presence to tell their story, and they found climbing the learning curve too difficult and too slow. HAX to the rescue!  

[**Customer Discovery 101.**](https://invent.psu.edu/programs/customer-discovery-101/) Annie Hughes, Statewide Coordinator with Invent Penn State Launchbox and Innovation Network: “We reached out to HAX while searching for a platform to develop Customer Discovery 101, a new entrepreneurship training program available to the public. We were running into challenges finding systems that were flexible and customizable and also accessible for our end users. Our team had limited design experience and so needed an LMS that was user-friendly and intuitive…our program has received overwhelmingly positive reviews from our users for both functionality and design.”

[**Digital Beaumont and Fletcher (1647).**](https://openpublishing.psu.edu/digital-beaumont-fletcher-about-project) *Digital Beaumont & Fletcher* is a collaborative, open-access editorial project that aims to make the rarely edited—and, as a result, rarely taught—plays in the “Beaumont and Fletcher” canon accessible to student readers and freely available online for classroom use. All texts are prepared by undergraduate and graduate students.

**Online Learning.** At Penn State, HAX supports major online course portfolios in the College of Arts and Architecture and the Eberly College of Science. Many thousands of students at University Park and the World Campus have relied on HAX-delivered courses for online learning.

**Continuous Improvement through Community Involvement.** Literally, not a single day goes by that HAX is not improved. **The best part:** undergraduate students, both at Penn State and at Georgia Tech, are readily able to contribute functionality enhancements to HAX. This is a tremendous win-win arrangement. Students enhance their resumes and portfolios while learning to produce production-ready code incorporated into a real system, and HAX capabilities and usability increase quickly.

## Web Publishing Unlike All Else

This is the hardest message for us to communicate. What makes HAX unique compared to well-known web authoring tools like WordPress, Squarespace, or Wix? Nothing answers that question like first-hand authoring with HAX, but here are just a few major differences:

- **Free and Open:** Open documentation, open APIs, open community, content moves freely into AND out of HAX; $0 cost, zero risk, everything generates static files
- **Web component standard:** Built from non-proprietary components that will never cease to function; your content is “future-proof”
- **Unparalleled ease of use**: Build a web site by importing your existing files and site content (Word, PowerPoint, Excel, Adobe PDF, WordPress, Drupal, and more). Download sites as a zip, unpack them to migrate.
- **True WYSIWYG editor:** As familiar as the common text editor; build pages block-by-block and write perfect HTML without ever knowing it
- **Accessible content:** The best time to make content accessible is the moment it is created; HAX automates that invisibly
- **Fully extensible: **Everything about HAX is “non-monolithic”; the ecosystem continues to grow and contributions are welcome
- **Platform-Agnostic:** HAX-generated content works anywhere, whether standalone or as part of a larger system; no hosting-specific backend constraints
- **Maximum remixability: **Remix and rapid prototype your site; duplicate an existing site, switch themes on the fly, change the content structure at a site or page level
- **Sustainable:** The HAX ecosystem itself, and the community surrounding it, are highly sustainable; vendors vanish, but an open source ecosystem can live indefinitely
- **AI Forward:** HAX has SKILLS that make it easy for developers to use AI to install and enhance the platform. All sites made with HAX also automatically manage API, file structure and output formats that enhance discoverability, reuse and interrogation by AI agents.
